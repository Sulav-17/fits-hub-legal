Privacy Policy — Fits Hub

Last updated: August 2026

Fits Hub is a personal wardrobe app currently in testing. This policy explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

  • Account email address (for sign-in)
  • Photos you upload of your own garments and outfits
  • Photos you upload of yourself, for outfit visualization features
  • Photos of outfits worn by others that you choose to upload for reference

How this information is used

  • To provide the app's core features: organizing your closet, generating outfit suggestions, and generating visualizations of outfits on your photo
  • Photos are processed through third-party AI image services (including FASHN) solely to power these features
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers

Data storage and deletion

  • Your data is stored securely and is accessible only to your account
  • You can delete individual photos and generated results directly within the app
  • To request full account deletion, contact us at digitalaiproducts@gmail.com

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact digitalaiproducts@gmail.com
